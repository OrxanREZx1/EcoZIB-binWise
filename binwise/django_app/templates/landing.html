The problem

Traditional waste collection is inefficient

Fixed schedules, lack of real-time data, and invisible safety risks cost time, money, and public trust.

Overflowing bins
Fixed collection schedules often miss bins that become full too early, causing overflow and complaints.
Unnecessary trips
Trucks visit half-empty bins, wasting fuel, labor, and time that could be better spent elsewhere.
No real-time visibility
Operators have no way to know which bins need urgent collection until it's too late.
Safety risks
Fires or abnormal heat inside bins can go unnoticed and escalate into serious incidents.
Our solution

Connected bins. Smarter decisions.

BinWise turns ordinary litter bins into smart connected bins. Sensors measure fill level and temperature, then send data to a web dashboard where operators can monitor status, detect risks, and make better collection decisions.

What we offer

Everything operators need

From real-time monitoring to fire-risk detection, BinWise gives your team the visibility to act fast and plan smart.

Real-time fill monitoring
Track exactly how full each bin is using ultrasonic distance sensing, updated every 30 seconds.

Fire risk detection
